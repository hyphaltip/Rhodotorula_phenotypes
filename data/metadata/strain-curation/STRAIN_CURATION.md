# Strain curation table
Generated by `scripts/curation/build_strain_curation.py` (run through SLURM). Do not edit the CSV; edit the rules in the script and rerun.

- `strain_curation.csv`: 321 rows (one per phenotyped strain). Columns include the corrected `species`, `ploidy_status`, `hybrid_subgroup`, popgen group membership, identity flags, `gwas_exclude_reason`, `gwas_panel` and de-clone groups at 2, 5, 10, 20 and 50 SNPs. One column beyond the approved list: `popgen_strain` (matched popgen spelling, for traceability).
- `strain_curation_changes.csv`: every species, marker and ploidy change with old value, new value, reason, decision id and evidence.
- Results (2026-10-08): species changes 13 (3 from D-37, 5 from D-45, 5 from D-46); 7 strains marked `aff_mucilaginosa`; ploidy 185 haploid, 33 diploid_hybrid, 5 diploid_other, 98 unknown (not in the callset, mostly other species); GWAS panel 126 strains at the 5-SNP cutoff (equals the D-49 expectation; the script stops if it differs). No panel strain has an identity flag, a non-haploid status or a marker.
- DH4148 is coded haploid and excluded from the panel (no genotypes, D-54). Strain ids 165 and 269 share a sample name and are flagged; they have no genome.
- `population_*` columns are empty until the DH4148 SNP clusters exist (D-51).
