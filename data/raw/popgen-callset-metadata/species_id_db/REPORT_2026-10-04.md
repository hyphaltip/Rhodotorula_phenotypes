| Scoring | `scripts/call_species.py` | Per query and label: mean of the top 3 ANI values |

### Scoring rules

**Hybrid references are excluded.** A hybrid diploid assembly holds two subgenomes but carries one label. Without the exclusion, the hybrids labelled *R. mucilaginosa* pulled pure *aff. mucilaginosa* and *frigidialcoholis* queries toward *R. mucilaginosa*. The excluded references are:
- the 43 strains marked diploid in `ploidy_overrides.csv`
- every `_hap` assembly

**ANI metric.**
- Read queries use `match_containment_ani`, the share of a reference's k-mers found in the reads. Sequencing-error k-mers do not affect it.
- Assembly queries use `max_containment_ani`.

**Self-matches are excluded.** A query is never scored against a reference from the same strain.

## Limits

- The reference labels come from a phylogeny that may predate some genomes. Five references look mislabelled (table above).
- Labels with one reference cannot be validated. These are: *R. taiwanensis*, *R. babjevae*, *R. pacifica*, *R. evergladensis*, *R. araucariae*, *Curvibasidium* and sp.4.
- Scores are k-mer containment ANI estimates (k=31, scaled=1000), not alignment ANI. Values above 0.99 are reliable for ranking. Near 0.93 and below, they are less precise.
- A hybrid cannot be told apart from a mixed culture with these data alone.
