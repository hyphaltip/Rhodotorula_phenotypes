### [2026-08-15] D-7 — Use Mantel permutation as the primary phylogenetic-signal statistic; keep K/lambda as caveated references
- **Context**: Idea 09 tested strain color/growth traits for phylogenetic signal on the PHYling protein tree (`rhodotorula-phyling-protein-tree`, 278 tips, ~216 R. mucilaginosa). The tree is a near-comet: 167/541 edges ≤1e-7 and 22 zero-length pendant edges form a huge polytomy of near-duplicate mucilaginosa genomes. On it Blomberg's K returned ≈1e-7 for every trait (and reproduced K≈2.25 for simulated BM data on the same tree — clearly degenerate), while likelihood lambda via geiger fitContinuous was internally inconsistent (white lnL exceeded lambda lnL); only phytools phylosig lambda (λ≈0.83–0.94) was directionally sane.
- **Decision**: Make a rank-based Mantel permutation test (Spearman on pairwise patristic distance vs absolute trait distance, 999 perms, seed 7) the primary statistic in `idea_09_phylogeny.R`; report Blomberg's K and Pagel's lambda only as reference columns (`_ref`) with a loud documented caveat that K is degenerate on this topology; compute both all-strains and within-R. mucilaginosa scopes.
- **Alternatives considered**: Report K/lambda as-is (correct-looking but topologically meaningless); drop all stats and keep only the tree figures; use only the lambda LRT (numerically unstable on this tree).
- **Rationale**: Permutation Mantel makes no normality/likelihood assumptions and is robust to the near-comet shape; it resolved K≈0 vs lambda≈0.9 into a coherent "weak-to-moderate, mostly between-species signal" (size r=0.43, heterogeneity r=0.31, chroma r=0.19, Cu slope r=0.10; all p≤0.003; pace n.s.). Keeping K/lambda as flagged references preserves diagnostics without misleading conclusions.
- **Consequences**: FINDING F6 (signal present) + methodology learning L-14; `idea09_phylo_signal.csv` columns mantel_r_spearman/mantel_p_perm/blomberg_K_ref/pagel_lambda_ref/pagel_lambda_p_ref. Future phylogeny analyses on near-comet/genome-cluster trees should follow the same rule (Mantel > raw K).
- **Tags**: phylogenetics, mantel, blomberg-K, pagel-lambda, permutation, near-comet, analysis-method, decision

### [2026-08-15] D-3 — Stratify plate-effect variance partition by Copper concentration
- **Context**: `analysis/explore_plate_position/01_variance_partition.R` estimated a pooled mixed model (`trait ~ copper_mm + grid_row_c + grid_col_c + is_edge + (1|strain_code) + (1|run_number) + (1|plate_id)`) reporting 23% (L*) and 33% (b*) plate-explained variance. - **Rationale**: Stratification removes the plate↔Cu confound directly and yields per-condition plate variance estimates, which are the quantities of interest given the stress gradient (0 mM control vs 30 mM).
- **Consequences**: Stratified plate variance is much smaller (L* ~0.1-4.7%, b* 0-3.2%) than the pooled 23%/33% — the pooled numbers were substantially an artifact of pooling across concentrations with a non-linear Cu response. Outputs renamed to `*_by_copper.csv`; pooled files (`variance_components.csv`, `fixed_effects.csv`, lmer_models_linear/categorical.rds) deleted. **ID: 2026-08-16 D-9 — Use the kinship-only GEMMA LMM as the primary GWAS; park PC/population-covariate models**
- **Decision**: Report the GWAS association results from the **kinship-only** unified linear mixed model (`gemma -lmm 4 -k kins`, no explicit structure covariates) as the primary/valid analysis for this panel. Expert quantitative-geneticist review (08b) concurs and recommends kinship/LOCity alone (not PC covariates) for this structure.

The conservative lambda is a structural model diagnostic, not a biology verdict.

### [2026-08-16] D-11 — Tier-C significance: use Benjamini-Hochberg FDR, NOT maxT phenotype-permutation
- **Context**: Tier-C completion needed an honest genome-wide threshold (design rec #4). GEMMA 0.98.3 has no `--pheno-permute`. I built a manual maxT null (permute fam col-6, rerun `-lmm 4`, record min-p; 1000 perms × chroma/AUC_10/resilience_30 on the LD-pruned set).

Such a threshold would reject every real hit. FDR (BH, q=0.05) is robust, needs no new compute, and is explicitly endorsed by expert rec #4 and the design doc (line 119). ### [2026-08-17] D-14 — Tier D/E/G: annotate loci→genes (250 kb clump), ABF fine-map in z-space, and treat prior growth-rate locus replication as a rare-EF proxy test
- **Context**: Divergence review (`09-NEXT-GWAS-DESIGN.md` §7) approved three follow-ups after Tier C/FDR: (D) map FDR-sig + BSLMM loci to genes for a biological readout; (E) fine-map the anchors into credible sets with effect-size confidence; (G) check whether the prior lab's growth-rate top hit (chr13:13_30149, p=1.68e-11) replicates in our color/copper panel.
- **Why**: Closing the gap from "SNP p-values" to "genes + credible sets" is the difference between a GWAS a geneticist can act on and a list. - **Decisions**: (1) Independent loci = positional 250 kb clump keyed on the single most-significant SNP per chromosome (keeps one lead/region per chr — documented caveat: a second distant block on the same small scaffold collapses); (2) Fine-mapping = Wakefield ABF computed in **z-space** with prior SD=0.2 on the NCP, logsumexp-normalized PIPs, candidates filtered to p<1e-3 (trait-scale priors break across heterogeneous trait units; unfiltered sets span thousands of nulls); (3) BSLMM loci annotated via a `PIP<num>` regex (their CSV is unquoted-comma malformed → read with `csv.reader`); (4) Replication = closest-genotyped-SNP proxy test per trait (exact pos 30149 was MAF-filtered out of our panel); (5) gene index built from mRNA-derived genes (6,799), keyed `scaffold_N`, with FDR-table int-chr→`scaffold_<int>` mapping.
- **Effect**: Tier D → 12,348 FDR-sig SNPs, 5,286 independent loci, 8 BSLMM loci (telomerase RT, DBP3 ATPase, methionine aminopeptidase 1 among hits). Tier E → chroma scaffold_10 95% CS n=24 (lead pp=0.054, common AF) is the paper-grade anchor; common vs rare-EF (af≈0.015) split matches BSLMM's near-oligogenic call. **Tier G → prior growth-rate locus DOES replicate in our AUC_10** (proxy 13_30134, p=4.03e-6 FDR-sig, same chr13 block), gene = OM429_005439 (hypothetical, unannotated).

### [2026-08-27] D-29 — Orthogonal rare-variant validation (population-stratified exact carrier permutation): scaffold_9:704260 stays unconfirmed, scaffold_13:810026 gets independent support
- **Context**: D-25/D-27 established that this project's population-vs-locus battery structurally cannot validate a rare single-SNP hit (requires ≥2-population replication, unreachable for a 2-5-carrier allele), leaving `scaffold_9:704260` and `scaffold_13:810026` both "unconfirmed" with no way to move either forward. User asked for a described-then-implemented orthogonal strategy.
- **Decision**: Built `scripts/rare_variant_carrier_permutation_test.py` — take the real carriers' population-membership counts as a fixed stratum, exactly enumerate every possible same-stratified pseudo-carrier draw (combinatorially exhaustive, not Monte Carlo, since counts are small: up to 61,446 combinations), and report the real carriers' percentile in that exact null. This answers a genuinely different, valid question than §13's battery (extremity given exact population placement, not independent cross-population replication) and works even for a single-population-confined allele, at the cost of not being able to rule out fine-scale within-population relatedness.
- **Result**: `scaffold_9:704260` (cu_doseauc_v0151, 5 carriers all in one population, pop3): p=0.199, 92.4th percentile — does NOT pass; a second, methodologically independent negative result. `scaffold_13:810026` (resilience_30 AND AUC_30, 3 carriers spanning 2 distinct populations, pop1+pop6): p=0.0013 and p=0.0025, 99.87th/99.75th percentile — passes decisively for both traits.
- **Rationale**: The test's design deliberately uses the exact observed carrier population placement as its null's stratification, so it can't be gamed by population-baseline differences the way a naive carriers-vs-everyone comparison could; that it gives a clearly negative answer for one locus and a clearly positive one for the other (rather than uniformly "significant") is itself evidence the test has real discriminating power, not just default significance.
- **Consequences**: `scaffold_13:810026` now has independent support that isn't confounded by "every prior check shares the same carrier strains" (D-27's core critique) — promote it above `scaffold_9:704260` in confidence, though still not to the standard of a `likely_real` §13 verdict (this test cannot rule out fine-scale within-population relatedness as the true driver, a real remaining gap for a 3-carrier, spanning-2-population signal). `scaffold_9:704260` should be treated as unconfirmed on two independent grounds now, not one.
